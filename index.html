Comfortable, well equipped, and everything you need

We offer modern accommodation in a quiet building, with flats across three storeys, an ideal choice for group and long-term stays, or for corporate travellers passing through.

On the first floor, a separate apartment for three people includes a kitchen and a private bathroom with shower and toilet.

The second floor and the attached attic hold three separate double rooms. The attic has its own private bathroom with shower and toilet. The second floor also has a spacious kitchen with a dining table, a television, and a private bathroom with a bathtub and toilet, along with a utility room with a washing machine and dryer.

The attic and second floor can be adapted to suit individual requirements, for up to ten people.
