A quiet retreat

Rest in comfort beneath rustic wooden beams

Kamenka offers spacious, comfortable accommodation in a characterful historic building in Klášterec nad Ohří. With exposed brick, heavy wooden beams and plenty of room to settle in, it is well suited to corporate travellers and longer work stays, as well as families, friends and visitors exploring the surrounding area.

Open space with character

A former warehouse from the age of Bohemia’s great porcelain makers, Kamenka has been thoughtfully converted into warm, rustic accommodation. White walls, exposed brick and substantial wooden beams give the space its distinctive character, while the open-plan living and dining area leads out onto a balcony overlooking the surroundings.

We offer modern accommodation in a quiet building with flats across three storeys, making it an ideal choice for a group, for long-term stays, or for corporate travellers passing through.

On the first floor, a separate apartment for three people includes a kitchen and a private bathroom with a shower and a toilet.

Comfortable, well equipped, and everything you need

The second floor and the attached attic hold three separate double rooms. The attic has its own private bathroom with a shower and a toilet. The second floor also has a spacious kitchen with a dining table, a television, and a private bathroom with a bathtub and a toilet, along with a utility room with a washing machine and dryer.

The attic and second floor can be adapted to suit individual requirements, for up to ten people.
