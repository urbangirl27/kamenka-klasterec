A FEW HOUSE RULES

A Few House Rules

To keep Kamenka clean, comfortable and welcoming for everyone, we kindly ask our guests to observe a few simple rules during their stay.

No smoking indoors

Smoking is not permitted inside the flat. Thank you for helping us keep the indoor environment fresh and pleasant for all guests.

Footwear and cleanliness

Please be mindful of muddy or very dirty shoes when entering and walking around the flat. Kindly remove your shoes or clean them before coming inside to help keep the floors clean.

Food and Cleanliness

Please do not leave food outside the flat or unattended on the stovetop. Leftover food can attract mice, other rodents, flies and fruit flies into the building. Kindly store food properly and keep the kitchen clean to help prevent unwanted pests.

Cleanliness and Excessive Waste

Guests are kindly asked to leave the flat in a reasonable state of cleanliness and dispose of their rubbish appropriately. An additional fee of 1,000 CZK will be charged if excessive rubbish is left behind or the flat requires exceptional cleaning due to its condition.

Registered Guests and Occupancy

Please inform us of the total number of guests who will be staying at the flat when making your booking. This allows us to prepare the accommodation appropriately and ensure everyone is comfortably accommodated. Any additional guests who have not been declared in advance may incur an extra charge. Please contact us beforehand if the number of guests changes.

Property Damage

Guests are responsible for any damage to the flat, its furnishings, equipment or other property caused during their stay. Please report any accidental damage to us as soon as possible. The cost of repairing or replacing damaged items may be charged to the guest responsible.
