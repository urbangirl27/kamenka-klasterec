Explore the surroundings of Kamenka

Discover Klášterec nad Ohří

Explore the Ohře River while visiting Klášterec nad Ohří Chateau and its beautiful surrounding park. The town and its surroundings offer a peaceful setting to explore, unwind and enjoy the landscape.

What is nearby

For everyday essentials, Lidl and Tesco are both approximately 300 metres from Kamenka. A pizzeria and bistro are also nearby, making it easy to find something to eat without going far.
